  • Working Hours: Slots are offered strictly between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM.

  • Cancellation Token: Upon booking, a private security token is generated for you. Keep this token safe to cancel or reschedule your slot.
  • Re-opening Slots: If you cancel your appointment, the slot is made available for others right away.
