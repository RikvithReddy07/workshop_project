Frequently Asked Questions

How does duplicate booking prevention work?
Our platform uses an atomic database constraint (partial unique index) combined with SQLite immediate locking. Even if two people click "Book" for the exact same date and time slot simultaneously, only the first request succeeds; the second request receives a clear message that the slot is already taken.
What is a cancellation token and why do I need it?
A cancellation token is a secure 32-byte cryptographic code issued exclusively to you upon booking. It protects your appointment from unauthorized tampering or fraudulent cancellation. When cancelling, providing this token verifies your ownership of the booking.
Can I book an appointment for a past date?
No. Both the client-side calendar selector and backend verification strictly prohibit past dates. You can book starting from today and any future business date.
What happens after an appointment is cancelled?
The appointment record is preserved with a "Cancelled" status for audit and record purposes, and the database constraint is lifted so that the slot immediately reopens for anyone else to reserve.
How do I look up my scheduled appointments?
Visit the My Appointments page and type the email address you used during reservation into the lookup bar. All past and upcoming bookings matching that email will be displayed.
